About me

Background, education, honours and the tools I use.

I am a Level 4 undergraduate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at BUET, graduating in 2027. My thesis, supervised by Dr. Mohammad Abdul Motalab, uses atomistic simulation to understand the reliability of solder joints in electronic packaging.

Before simulation, I learned engineering by building: a honeycomb thermal storage unit that grew from a course project into a conference paper, rover subsystems for BUET's Mars rover team, and two robots. I like problems where careful modelling and physical hardware meet.

Outside the lab, I lead the ASHRAE BUET Student Branch and the IMechE BUET Student Chapter and serve as Secretary of the BUET Automobile Club. I am looking for graduate research opportunities in computational mechanics and thermal sciences.
