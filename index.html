Undergraduate thesis, in progress

Atomistic reliability of Cu/SAC305/Cu solder joints

Jul 2026 to present. Supervisor: Dr. M. A. Motalab

Molecular dynamics of SAC305 solder with its Ag3Sn and Cu6Sn5 precipitates built as discrete particles, to see how microstructure changes the response to monotonic and cyclic shear. Next: the full layered copper, intermetallic and solder stack.

Honeycomb latent heat thermal energy storage

Oct 2025 to present. Dept. of ME, BUET

A paraffin storage unit in honeycomb cells for solar-assisted hot water. I designed it, led the prototype build and its pressure test, ran transient CFD of melting and solidification, and wrote the paper as corresponding author.
