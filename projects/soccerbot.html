SoccerBOT, a robot that moves sideways

A remote-controlled soccer robot on mecanum wheels. It can drive forward, slide sideways, cut diagonally and spin on the spot, which is exactly what you want when the ball is loose.

My roleChassis design, assembly lead and match driver
ResultTop five, BUET Robo Carnival 2024
Competed atBUET Robo Carnival 2024 and UAP Techtron 2025
ToolsSolidWorks, AutoCAD, PCB work

The wheels01

Why mecanum wheels.

Each wheel carries small rollers set at 45 degrees. Spin the four wheels in different combinations and the angled forces add up to motion in any direction, without steering. Pick a move.

The build02

Built in layers.

A low, stiff chassis carries the drive. Everything else stacks on top of it.

  1. 1
    Mecanum wheelsFour wheels with angled rollers, one at each corner.
  2. 2
    Chassis and gear motorsA plate chassis with a geared DC motor driving each wheel.
  3. 3
    Ball guideBent sheet-metal plates at the front that cradle and push the ball.
  4. 4
    Control deckMicrocontroller, motor drivers and the radio receiver, wired on an upper deck.
  5. 5
    Hand-held radioA hobby radio transmitter drives the robot in real time.

Versions03

Then closed it up.

The first robot was an open frame. For the next competition it got an enclosed body over the electronics.

2024Version 1, open frameFast to build and easy to rewire between matches. Finished in the top five at BUET Robo Carnival 2024.
2025Version 2, enclosed bodyThe wiring sits inside a closed shell. Competed in the SoccerBOT segment of UAP Techtron 2025.
